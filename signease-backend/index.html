How it works

A simple four-step communication flow.

1

Show your sign

Position your hand in front of the camera.

2

Camera captures

The browser accesses your camera stream.

3

Recognize

The future ML model identifies the gesture.

4

Translate

The result appears as text and can be spoken aloud.
